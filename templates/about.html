About JamSplitter

JamSplitter is a powerful, AI-powered tool that separates audio tracks into individual stems, such as vocals, drums, bass, and instruments. It's perfect for musicians, producers, DJs, and music enthusiasts who want to remix, study, or create new versions of their favorite songs.

✨ Key Features

🎵 Advanced Stem Separation

Separate audio into multiple stems including vocals, drums, bass, and other instruments using state-of-the-art AI models.

⚡ GPU Acceleration

Lightning-fast processing with NVIDIA CUDA and AMD ROCm support for maximum performance.

🎧 Multiple Formats

Support for various audio formats including MP3, WAV, and FLAC to suit your needs.

🌐 Easy to Use

Simple and intuitive web interface with real-time processing status and progress tracking.

🔌 RESTful API

Full-featured API for programmatic access and integration with your applications.

🎬 YouTube Support

Process audio directly from YouTube videos by simply pasting the URL.

🚀 How It Works

  1. 1

    Enter a YouTube URL

    Paste a YouTube video URL into the input field on the home page.

  2. 2

    AI Processing

    Our advanced AI models analyze and separate the audio into individual stems.

  3. 3

    Download Your Stems

    Once processing is complete, download the separated tracks in your preferred format.

  • Learning: Study individual instrument parts and improve your skills
